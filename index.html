What is XMRT DAO?

XMRT DAO is a decentralized autonomous organization focused on Monero mining. We combine custom hardware (XMRT Charger), smart contract mining (MESHNET), and an agent-driven operational layer to create the most accessible mining ecosystem in the world.

Our fleet of autonomous agents — Vex, Hermes, Eliza, and others — handle operations, outreach, content, and coordination across a distributed network of miners and community members.
